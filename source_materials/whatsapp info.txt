Minibio:

Otávio Bopp is an economist from Fundação Getúlio Vargas. He has worked has a researcher at IBRE and as an advisor for municipal councilman Flávio Valle, before working at SMDEIS. Bopp thrives in the intersection between public policies, academic research and innovative technologies.

What will be presented:

This presentation will talk about the municipal level strategies that allowed great economic development in the city of Rio de Janeiro in the aftermath of the 2015 Brazilian economic crisis and the COVID-19 Pandemic, given the constraints of the federative pact. We will talk about how we drove "inclusive economic growth" in recent years — helping firms of different size and sectors, while lowering innequalities — how  how this allowed the city of Rio to recover faster than Brazil and the state of Rio de Janeiro as a whole and how empiracal data was used to drive this growth in different axis.
